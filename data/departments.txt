1-Research Management Cell (RMC)
In meeting its research goals the College may find it advantageous to establish formal
organizational structures to promote, facilitate, coordinate and collaborate all the Research
activities. The RMC at the College should establish within the context of this policy. No other
structure may be styled a research unit of the College unless it has been properly constituted
according to this policy. The objective of creating this entity is to improve the quality,
quantity, and visibility of the College’s research output, and to provide a better research
environment for the faculties and students. They are intended to enhance the standing of the
College as a pre-eminent research institution, benefit from the national and international
funding agencies and collaboration, by building up significant bodies of expertise at the
College, increase the level of interaction between researchers, College units and College
management, develop policies and procedures in line with the research ethos of the College,
and provide effective management of research.

2-Placement Cell
Our Placement Cell is meant to help students build on and improve the abilities they’ve mastered in their studies. Employers with MoU signed with us, completely support students’ growth, evaluate their progress, and strive to create a positive learning atmosphere where students may exchange new ideas and expand their computational knowledge. The placement cell assists students in locating appropriate placement options. The support starts with CV preparation and interview methods and it also develops various soft skills that are essential for a professional career.

3- Local Committee IAESTE
Kantipur City College is an official partner of IAESTE, the International Association for the Exchange of
Students for Technical Experience. IAESTE is an association of national committees representing
academic, industrial and student interests.
We serve 4000 students, 3000 employers and 1000 academic institutions through career-focused
professional internships abroad, social and intercultural reception programs, international networking
and other career and employer branding activities in 86 countries worldwide.
In IAESTE we are committed to fostering international understanding and impacting on the development
of young people through international exchange. Our core values of friendship, trust, respect,
professionalism, development and intercultural cooperation serve as a foundation for our strong
organizational culture and as the guiding principles for all our work and daily activities.
The local committee of IAESTE Nepal is stationed right here at Kantipur City College. Students interested
to participate in IAESTE can contact the local committee directly or inquire at the Department for
International Affairs.

4- Department of International Relationships
The Department of International Affairs at Kantipur City College is the responsible body for all matters
related to maintaining, expanding and seeking international partnerships and projects. The team of the
Department of International affairs advises students, academics and staff who are planning a visit
abroad, as well as foreign students who wish to study at Kantipur City College. It strives to expose
students of Kantipur City College to different countries and cultures around the world and to foster a
spirit for international collaboration in order to prepare them for working in an increasingly globalized
world.

The Department of International Affairs at Kantipur City College coordinates and manages all
international relationships, both to partner universities and businesses abroad. It coordinates exchange
programs and projects, supports both outgoing and incoming exchange students and strives to
strengthen the international spirit of everyone studying or working at Kantipur City College.

Activities of the department

In detail, our activities include:

Support for outgoing students: Students of Kantipur City College who want to go abroad for studying or an internship can contact the Department of International Affairs for advise on organization, travel, visa, insurance, and funding.
Support for incoming students: Students from foreign countries who want to come to study at Kantipur City College can also reach out to the Department of International Affairs for any support they need.
The Department of International Affairs assists teachers and other faculty members who want to go abroad. We advise on different programs both in Asia and other continents and provide information on funding possibilities.
It is the main responsibility of the Office of International Affairs to establish and maintain long- term and mutually beneficial partnerships with universities and businesses abroad.
Representatives from foreign universities and businesses are welcome to contact the Office of International Affairs if they want to collaborate on a program or project or have any other ideas for working together.
